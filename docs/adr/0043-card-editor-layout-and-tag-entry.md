# 43. The card editor's layout, and the tag entry

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-17
- Amends: ADR 0022 (point 6, the layout and the preview toggle), ADR 0031 (the tags field is pills, not a comma-separated entry)
- Refers to: ADR 0042 (tag pills), `docs/briefs/phase-1-finish.md` ("Editor", "Tags", "Design rules")

## Context

The editor showed Front, Back and Tags as headings over their fields, a comma-separated tags entry, a header toggle for the preview, and the preview below the text on narrow windows, where it took height the text needed. The phase 1 brief asks for sections laid out like a preferences page, tags as pills with an entry for adding, the preview beside the text when wide and a toggle between them when narrow (not stacked), and a quiet "Ctrl+Enter to save" hint. A later slice reuses the tag entry in the All Cards "Add Tag…" dialog, so it had to be its own widget.

## Decision

### Layout

- **Front, Back and Tags are `AdwPreferencesGroup`s** (title in libadwaita's group style; Tags has the description "Press Enter or type a comma after each tag", the one thing about the field that is not obvious). They sit in an `AdwClamp` (720, tightening from 600), 24 px apart, 12 px from the edges. The two `GtkSourceView`s keep their `GtkFrame` (ADR 0022) and share the extra height. Their accessible labels are now "Front" and "Back" directly, since a group's title is not a widget to point at.
- **Wide: the preview is beside the text, with no toggle to hide it.** The old header toggle existed because the preview below the text took height; beside it, it costs nothing the text needs, it is debounced and cut at 32 KiB (ADR 0022), and dropping it leaves Cancel, the title and Save in the header (the brief's limit of about three controls per side). A wider window shows both; there is no setting.
- **Narrow (under 720 sp, the dialog's `AdwBreakpoint`): an Edit / Preview `AdwToggleGroup`** (libadwaita 1.7) **in a bottom `GtkActionBar`** shows one pane at a time. The HIG puts view switchers in a bottom bar on narrow windows; there it is in reach of a thumb, and the header keeps its title and two buttons at 360 px. The panes for each case are the pure `layout::panes`; the breakpoint's `apply` / `unapply` (which run after its setters) set `narrow`. Alt+E and Alt+P switch from the keyboard. The focus never stays where keys go nowhere (the pure `layout::focus_after`, read before the panes change): switching to Preview with the focus in the text moves it to the toggle, and when the dialog grows wide while the focus is on the toggle (now hidden), or the focus is lost, it goes to the front. Ctrl+Shift+Enter always returns to Edit. The preview is rendered only while on screen, as before.
- **The cut note is the preview group's description**, in libadwaita's dimmed style, instead of a separate caption.
- **Save hint: an `AdwShortcutLabel`** (libadwaita 1.8, keycaps drawn by libadwaita) **for Ctrl+Enter, then "to save"**, in a `.dimmed` box, small (`.caption`), after the fields. It scrolls with them, so it never takes room from the text at 360 px, and in Preview mode it is hidden with the text. The Save button's tooltip still says "Save (Ctrl+Enter)".

### The tag entry (`widgets/tag_entry.rs`)

- **One frame holding the pills and a bare `GtkText` after them**, in the pills' wrapping lines (`TagPills` in its wrapping form, with the new `removable` and `end_child`). The frame has the text-view background (`--view-bg-color`), like the two text fields above, so the three read as one form; a click on its empty part focuses the text. The text's placeholder is "Add a tag", dimmed as an entry's would be. Under the frame, the field's error in `.error .caption`.
- **A removable pill** is a box with the `.tag-pill` look holding the name and a flat, circular 24 px remove button (`window-close-symbolic`), focusable, tooltip "Remove Tag", accessible label "Remove tag NAME" (pure `keys::remove_label`). Names stay plain labels (untrusted, ADR 0025).
- **A comma or Enter turns the typed name into a pill** (`field::split_typed`). The comma is acted on just after the text changes, at `G_PRIORITY_HIGH_IDLE`, before GDK's redraw priority, so it is never drawn, and pasting "a, b, c" or typing on an on-screen keyboard works the same as typing. Spaces before a name (after a comma, or typed into the empty text) are dropped as they are typed. Blank names, and names already a pill in any spelling (by `name_key`), are dropped; the pill keeps its spelling. **A name that cannot be a tag** (over 256 bytes) **stays in the text** with its error ("This tag is too long …"), so nothing typed is lost; splitting what is left again changes nothing.
- **The name being typed counts as a tag** (`field::check`): Ctrl+Enter with "geo" typed saves "geo", and leaving the field (Tab past its pills, a click elsewhere) makes it a pill, so the field shows what a save writes. The focus controller is on the whole field, which GTK tells only when the focus leaves the hierarchy rooted at it, so moving between the text, the pills and the text's popovers (context menu, emoji chooser, parented to the text) is not a leave, and a Paste from the context menu still goes into the name being typed. `GtkEventControllerFocus` also reports `leave` when the window is deactivated (it handles `GTK_CROSSING_ACTIVE` as well as `GTK_CROSSING_FOCUS`, GTK 4.20), so the decision waits for an idle and is the pure `keys::should_split` (window still active, focus outside the field). "Tags changed" (Discard Changes?) compares `tag_keys` of pills plus typed name with the saved ones, as before.
- **Backspace in an empty text moves the focus to the last pill's remove button; Backspace or Delete there removes the pill.** Removing on the first Backspace is what some chip fields do, but a key that does nothing in an empty field should not delete without showing what it will delete; with the focus ring on the pill first, a second press removes it, and repeated presses walk back through the pills (`keys::after_removal`: Backspace goes to the pill before; a click, Enter, Space or Delete goes to the pill that took its place, else the last one, as `ui::focus::index_after_removal`; with none left, the text). The text announces "Tag NAME added" / "Tag NAME removed".
- **Tab goes Front → Back → the text → each pill's remove button → out**, through the widget's `focus` vfunc (`keys::tab_stop`). The text comes first although it is drawn after the pills: adding a tag is what the field is mostly for, and Shift+Tab from the text goes back to Back.
- **Suggestions keep ADR 0031's behaviour** (popover never takes the focus; Down / Up / Tab / Escape in the text's capture-phase controller; nothing highlighted at first; Tab takes the highlighted or first; the full typed name listed first), with one change: **Enter with nothing highlighted makes the typed name a pill** instead of only closing the list. A taken suggestion becomes a pill. The popover is parented to the frame, so it lines up under the whole field, and is presented again when its rows change, so it shrinks with them (its surface kept the old height under X11 otherwise).
- The editor passes the card's tags in (`set_tags`), the existing tags for suggestions (`set_all_tags`), and, when a card's tags cannot be read, `set_unavailable(message)`: the field is disabled, says why, and a save leaves the tags alone (ADR 0031's `None`).
- The decisions are pure and unit-tested: `widgets/tag_entry/field.rs` (moved from `card_editor/tags.rs`) and `widgets/tag_entry/keys.rs`. `card_editor/suggest.rs` is gone: the widget owns the suggestions.

No new dependency; the Cargo feature stays `v1_8` (`AdwToggleGroup` 1.7, `AdwShortcutLabel` 1.8, `AdwWrapBox` 1.7).

## Consequences

- ADR 0031's reasons against chips (a custom widget that screen readers handle poorly) are answered by plain parts: each pill is a label and a labelled button, the text has the label "Tags" and the autocomplete property, and additions and removals are announced. How Orca reads it is still for the maintainer to check.
- In Preview mode on a narrow window the preview cannot be scrolled from the keyboard (it is not focusable, ADR 0022). A follow-up could make the preview pane focusable there.
- The remove buttons are 24 px, below phase 2's 44 px touch target (SPEC §4.8); that audit comes with phone and touch.
- `TagEntry` is ready for the All Cards "Add Tag…" dialog; that dialog decides whether it wants one tag or several.
